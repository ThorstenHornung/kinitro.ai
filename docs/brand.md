# Brand

**For partners presenting solutions on kinitro.ai.** This page summarizes the kinitro corporate identity guide, version 1.1. Follow it whenever you show the kinitro name, logo or colours. The names and the logo are not licensed by the beta evaluation license. Ask kinitro before you use them.

## Brand architecture

**kinitro** is the umbrella brand. Two product lines sit below it.

| Line | Wordmark | Subject |
|---|---|---|
| kinitro consulting | **kinitro** *consulting* | Consulting, AI solutions, interim management |
| kinitro ai | **kinitro** *ai* | The agentic collaboration and solution platform |

In running text, write the product line as **kinitro.ai**.

Rules:

1. The umbrella brand is the sender. The line makes it more precise. In doubt, **kinitro** appears alone.
2. The suffix (`ai` or `consulting`) follows `kinitro` without a space. It is italic (skewX -8°), lighter in weight and in a different colour. It is never bold, never upper case and never larger than `kinitro`.
3. Never two lines in one wordmark. Never a line without the umbrella brand: `ai` or `consulting` never appear alone.
4. Both lines share one palette and one signet. There is no separate logo and no separate colour per line.
5. The tone is grown-up, calm and credible.

## The signet

Four spheres in a row. The ring grows from a thin outline on the left to a filled sphere on the right. It stands for an impulse passing through a Newton's cradle.

- Standard view: **white** spheres on **Aubergine `#7E3F66`**.
- Height: the height of the letter "o" in `kinitro`.
- Clear space: about one sphere diameter on all sides.
- Do not recolour outside the palette. Do not break the row from thin to full.

## The wordmark

- Font: **Manrope**.
- `kinitro`: Medium (500).
- Suffix: Light or Regular (300 to 400), italic (skewX -8°).

| Background | `kinitro` | Suffix |
|---|---|---|
| Light (white, `#F8F5F9`) | Aubergine `#7E3F66` | Silver `#7E848E` |
| Aubergine `#7E3F66` | White `#FFFFFF` | Light silver `#C9CED6` |

Every tone has a light and a dark version. On a dark background, use the light version.

## Colour roles

Choose the role first, then the tone.

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Surface | Aubergine | `#7E3F66` | Full backgrounds, signet ground, wordmark `kinitro` on light, active states |
| Text | Aubergine dark | `#221320` | Body text, headings and labels on a light background |
| Secondary | Silver | `#7E848E` | Suffix on light, secondary text, neutral accents |
| Secondary light | Light silver | `#C9CED6` | Suffix and secondary text on an Aubergine surface |
| Charts | Azure | `#2C6E8C` | Charts and data series only. Never as a signal colour. |
| Signal | Gold | `#9C7322` | Signals, hints, single highlighted values. Never as a surface. |
| Light | White | `#FFFFFF` | Light surfaces, signet and wordmark on Aubergine |
| Base | Light base | `#F8F5F9` | Light page background, quiet fills |

## Contrast

Measured to WCAG.

| Foreground | On white | On Aubergine `#7E3F66` |
|---|---|---|
| Aubergine dark `#221320` | 17.8 : 1 | 2.4 : 1 (do not use) |
| Aubergine `#7E3F66` | 7.5 : 1 | 1.0 : 1 (do not use) |
| Silver `#7E848E` | 3.8 : 1 (large text and brand elements only) | 2.0 : 1 (do not use) |
| Light silver `#C9CED6` | 1.6 : 1 (do not use) | 4.8 : 1 |
| White `#FFFFFF` | not applicable | 7.5 : 1 |

## Check rules

1. **Judge colours on a printout, not on a dark-mode screen.** Dark screens make dark tones look richer than paper does.
2. **Role before tone.** A surface colour is not a text colour.
3. **Every tone has a light and a dark version.** On dark backgrounds, switch Silver to Light silver and `kinitro` to White.
4. **Azure and gold are guests.** They appear in charts and at signal points. They are never the brand colour and never fill a surface.

## Using the logo file

The file [`assets/kinitro-ai-logo.png`](../assets/kinitro-ai-logo.png) shows the signet on its Aubergine block with the wordmark `kinitro ai`. It is made for light backgrounds.

- Use the file as it is. Do not recolour, stretch, crop, outline or add effects.
- Keep clear space of about one sphere diameter.
- Place it on white or `#F8F5F9`.
- Do not rebuild the wordmark in another font or weight.
- Do not combine it with another logo into a new mark.
- Show it only as agreed with kinitro.
